Alfred simulator

Generates a home's history as get_home snapshots. Choose what kind of home. To look at one day, open the 3D view.

What is in a simulated house

DeviceSourceWhat it does
Contact sensorSimulatedThe door opens after waking, on outings, and at a few random times.
PlugSimulatedOne appliance per house (a kettle, a coffee machine, a television…), used at that house's usual time.
LightSimulatedOn for a while after waking, and from the evening until bedtime.
Temperature sensorSimulatedTemperature and humidity follow a daily curve with a small random drift.
Camera, smoke sensor, hubConstantThey report, but their values never change.

Wake time, bedtime, appliance and outings are drawn at random for each house, vary a little every day, and are skipped on some days. Only the file's layout, the device names and the value words (alarm, normal) are copied from our real kit.

What is real in the real house

DeviceSourceWhat it is
Contact sensorRealEvery opening and closing of that house's fridge door, about 65 a day. It is not a front door: the fridge was the only door with a sensor.
LightRealThe kitchen lights: when they were switched on and off, and how bright.
Temperature sensorRealIndoor temperature and humidity, measured every 5 minutes.
PlugPartly realThe coffee maker (about 1,000 W for 7 minutes, on a timer at 06:30). Its meter only recorded 17 of the days. On the other days, one of its recorded uses is replayed at one of its recorded start times.
Camera, smoke sensor, hubConstantNot from that house. They keep the values of our own kit and never change.

The house is "Home A" of the UMass Smart* data set, in Massachusetts, recorded from May to July 2012. All the real values come from that same house, so the links between them are genuine. Two things are changed: the dates are moved to 2026, keeping each weekday and time of day, and temperatures are converted from °F to °C.
