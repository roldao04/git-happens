          Planted vs found

          What the simulator planted in this house (its answer key, never shown to the learners), and what Alfred found by itself from the devices' data.

          Habits

          Patterns that come back together in a rhythm (every day, on some weekdays, every other weekend, monthly or every few hours), with when they happen and how that varies.

          Occasions

          Rare days that depart from the usual one, such as a dinner with friends, recognised by how much they look like past ones.

          Heating

          How fast this house warms when the heating is on, and cools when it is off, measured from its own past heating. Alfred starts the heating this far ahead of a habit.

          Tomorrow

          What each habit will probably do in the 24 hours after this day, and what Alfred plans for it. From 75 % sure it acts (the lamp and the thermostat, within the guard's limits; it never switches a plug on). Below that it only suggests.

          Patterns

          Event types

          The kinds of things each device does, told apart by how big and how long they are. Patterns are made of these.
